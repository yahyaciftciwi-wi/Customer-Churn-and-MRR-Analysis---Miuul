# Customer Churn & MRR Segmentation Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
As the final case study of the Excel module in the Miuul Data Analytics Bootcamp, I conducted a customer behavior and risk segmentation analysis on raw transactional data. By evaluating **Churn Rate**, **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**, and **Tenure**, this project aims to identify high-risk cohorts and optimize customer retention strategies.

To effectively map multidimensional risk profiles, I utilized **Bubble Charts** to simultaneously visualize segment size, revenue value, lifetime duration, and churn severity in a single analytical view.

---

## 🔑 Key Analytical Insights & Takeaways

### 📉 1. Tenure vs. Churn Dynamics
* **High-Risk Onboarding:** Segments with short tenure (e.g., tenure ≤ 3 months such as `J_6AY_Not_Using_MM` with 52% churn) exhibit significantly higher churn rates.
* **Long-Term Stability:** Customers with longer tenure (e.g., `S_16+_Using_TY` with 66 months tenure) maintain low churn rates (~5%), underscoring the vital importance of early-stage onboarding and retention efforts.

### 💰 2. MRR Stability & Revenue At Risk
* **Revenue Anchor:** Higher MRR segments generally demonstrate lower churn rates and more stable retention patterns.
* **Critical Danger Zones:** Specific segments show high MRR alongside elevated churn (e.g., `S_6AY_Not_Using_MM` with 75% churn and high MRR), representing high-value revenue leakage that requires immediate targeted retention intervention.

### 🫧 3. Multidimensional Bubble Chart Visualization
* **Visual Framing:** Integrated 3D metrics where bubble size and position reflect customer lifetime length, revenue weight, and churn risk.
* **Strategic Value:** Enables executive teams to quickly isolate critical segments needing immediate intervention vs. loyal cohorts that anchor baseline revenue.
